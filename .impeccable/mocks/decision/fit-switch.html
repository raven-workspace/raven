What each part does
powershell
Starts PowerShell. One token, so no quoting breaks in cmd, a URL or a SQL string.
-nop
No profile, so nothing slows or alters the start.
-w hidden
No visible window on the target.
-e
Base64 of UTF-16LE text. Plain UTF-8 base64 produces garbage.
